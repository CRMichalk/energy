Wavelength, Frequency & Photon Energy

Learn the formulas first, then use the calculator to check your own work.

What you need to know

  • Nanometer conversion: 1 nm = 1 × 10−9 m
  • Speed of light: c = 3.00 × 108 m/s
  • Speed-of-light relationship: c = fλ
  • Frequency formula: f = c/λ
  • Planck's constant: h = 6.63 × 10−34 J·s
  • Planck's equation: E = hf

The basic path is: wavelength → meters → frequency → photon energy.

Step 1 — Convert nm to meters

The wavelength is usually given in nanometers (nm), but the speed-of-light formula requires wavelength in meters (m).

1 nm = 1 × 10−9 m

So multiply the wavelength in nm by 1 × 10−9:

λ(m) = λ(nm) × 1 × 10−9

Example structure using any number x:

x nm × (1 × 10−9 m / 1 nm) = x × 10−9 m
Important: The nm units cancel, leaving meters. This is why the conversion factor is written as a fraction.
Step 2 — Calculate frequency

Start with the formula in its theory form:

c = fλ

We want frequency, so solve for f:

f = c/λ

Now use the actual numbers:

f = (3.00 × 108 m/s) ÷ (your wavelength in meters)

After entering a wavelength above, the calculator will show the complete numerical substitution for you.

Units check: (m/s) ÷ m = 1/s, which is the same as Hz.
Step 3 — Calculate photon energy

Start with Planck's equation:

E = hf

Now substitute the known value of Planck's constant and your calculated frequency:

E = (6.63 × 10−34 J·s) × (your frequency in Hz)

The calculator will insert your actual frequency and show the numerical calculation.

Units check: J·s × 1/s = J.
Scientific notation — what happens to the exponents?

When multiplying

(a × 10x)(b × 10y) = (ab) × 10x+y

Add the exponents.

When dividing

(a × 10x) ÷ (b × 10y) = (a/b) × 10x−y

Subtract the exponents.

Watch negative exponents: 8 − (−7) = 8 + 7 = 15.

Moving the decimal

  • Move decimal left → exponent becomes one larger.
  • Move decimal right → exponent becomes one smaller.

Proper scientific notation has exactly one nonzero digit to the left of the decimal.

Complete worked structure

Every problem should follow this pattern:

1. CONVERT
λ(nm) × (1 × 10−9 m / 1 nm) = λ in meters
2. FREQUENCY
Theory: c = fλ
Solve for f: f = c/λ
Numbers: f = (3.00 × 108) ÷ (λ in meters)
3. ENERGY
Theory: E = hf
Numbers: E = (6.63 × 10−34) × (calculated frequency)

If she can write those three sections from memory, she has the basic process down.
